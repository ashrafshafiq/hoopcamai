Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Hoop Cam AI is designed to be private. Everything the app does happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Hoop Cam AI does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.

Camera and microphone

The app uses your device’s camera to detect the ball and players and to record video, and the microphone to record audio with your clips. This processing happens entirely on your device in real time. Camera and microphone data is used only to provide the app’s features and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Recordings

Videos you record are stored locally on your device (in the app’s folder, also visible in the Files app under “hoops-ai”). They remain under your control and are never uploaded by the app. If you choose to share or move a recording using iOS, that action is governed by the service you share it with.

Settings

Small preferences (such as your marked court area) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. They never leave your device.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
